## Recommended workflow

1. Start with the 11 supplier items above: ask the supplier for a shared folder or original image pack plus explicit permission to use it on your website.
2. Decide which of the 120 reference-derived products you will actually sell in the first launch. Do not migrate images for products you will not launch.
3. For each launch product, provide a folder of your original/licensed images. Name every folder with the product handle or final product title.
4. I will then create a local product-data file and connect each product page and collection card to only those local assets.

## What to send next

Any one of these is enough to start:

- a supplier Google Drive/Dropbox folder you are authorized to use;
- a ZIP/folder of original product images;
- a short list of the first 10–20 products you want to launch, so we only migrate those first.
